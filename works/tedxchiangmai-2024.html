TEDxChiangMai 2024

Year
2024
Duration
14 min

A talk at TEDxChiangMai 2024 on the question "How can we blend art and technology to shake up tradition?", told through projects with tomorrow.lab: the Midnight Rice festival at Wat Chomphu, the Himmapana workshop and Afterwish at the festival of the Inthakhin city pillar. Its thread: listen to what already exists, stay open to the new, and build a living culture at the same time.
