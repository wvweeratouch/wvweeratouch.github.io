Artwork

Poetry of the Unseen Tomorrow

Year
2022
Collaborators
COTH Studio
Medium
Hanging fabric strips, fluorescent ink, UV light, visitors' messages
Dimensions
Dimensions variable

The future remains uncertain and all our thoughts about it are merely conjectures. As humans move through time, planning for the future involves negotiating with space-time. In this exhibit, we invite you to contemplate your own “tomorrow” using a luminous pen.
With the shifting tones of ambient light, a simple phenomenon interacted with the highlighter marker. People enjoyed the message they expressed disappearing and reappearing as the light changed.
