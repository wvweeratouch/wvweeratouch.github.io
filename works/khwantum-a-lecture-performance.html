KHWANTUM — A Lecture Performance

Year
2026
Role
Creative Technologist
Collaborators
Nitipat Pholchai, Wendi Wu
Duration
40 min

KHWANTUM is a lecture performance where Southeast Asian ways of knowing meet shared imaginings of a future society. Three subjects that usually stay inside their own specialist vocabularies meet in one "field of possibility": quantum from science, AI from technology and business, and khwan from anthropology and spirituality. The audience is invited to play along. Created with Nitipat Pholchai and Wendi Wu and presented during the Arai-Eek Lab residency in Chiang Mai, with an exploratory version on Koh Phangan.
