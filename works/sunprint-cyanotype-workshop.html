Workshop

SUNPRINT Cyanotype Workshop

Year
2024
Duration
3 hrs

A cyanotype (sun-print) workshop for children and young people in the Happy School Break programme (ปิดเทอมสร้างสรรค์) in Chiang Mai, where participants made their own prints with sunlight. It ran three times, including an extra round opened on request and a pop-up at View From The Bus Tour's My Home Town Project concert.
