Artwork

The Oracle

Year
2026
Role
Creative Technologist
Collaborators
Henry Tan, Pongsakorn Wechakarn
Medium
Fog screen (ultrasonic foggers, fans, 3D-printed outlets), projection, Zoom, audio system
Dimensions
2.5 x 1 x 0.5 m

The Oracle is a fog-projection booth made for Emergence: The After Party, the launch of the Emergence catalog at York Manor, Los Angeles. Inside a dark tent, experts in synthetic biology and art appeared as faces floating in a sheet of mist, several of them joining remotely. Visitors placed a hand on an orb, an oracle appeared, and small groups asked questions about the future of technology and synthetic biology, often with prompt cards.
