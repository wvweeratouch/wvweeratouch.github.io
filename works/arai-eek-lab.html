Arai-Eek Lab

Year
2026
Collaborators
tomorrow.lab, Pa Rang Art Company, Hackteria, SGMK
Duration
6 weeks

A six-week art-science residency in Chiang Mai linking the Hackteria network and SGMK with tomorrow.lab and Pa Rang Art Company. Built on DIWO (Do-It-With-Others) practice, it brings ten artists-in-residence into a shared laboratory of biology, technology, and Lanna craft.
