Artwork

Observing & Being Observed

Year
2022
Medium
Microscope imaging system, projection mapping
Dimensions
Dimensions variable

According to modern science, “reality” is defined by separating the observer from the observed. However, as humans, we are related to everything in the world, including animals, trees, fungi, and microbes. This BioArt exhibition aims to explore the little world around us through a microscope and immerse you in an interactive visual experience where you become the observed entity. By disregarding scientific knowledge and relying on our senses, memory, and experience, we can fabricate new relationships with our world.
