ArtScience Entanglement Lab

Year

A workshop that invites people to help build a Quantum Art Archive for the region, using quantum ideas, wave-like behaviour and uncertainty, as a lens for making art through unusual interactive activities. Part of ArtScience Entanglement Lab, held at WiT in Bangkok 2026 with families and members of the public curious about science.
