Workshop

The Good Lab

Year
2024
Collaborators
Micro Galleries, Lanner, TEMPO.wav, Triple Edge

The Good Lab is a creative residency by Micro Galleries, in which artists make free, public art with and for local communities. Its Chiang Mai edition brought a public-art festival (living.cultures.fest) and the group exhibition ARTifacts: Art For The Future.
